How to read this. No protected attribute exists in the public data, so groups are inferred from the resume text (education level, experience band) and names are gender-coded test inputs, not a determination of anyone's gender. A gap in selection rate can reflect real differences in qualification; the equal-opportunity gap (how often truly relevant candidates are shortlisted, per group) is the fairer yardstick.
