## Section 2 - Your views
Create each statement as a *Linear scale* question, 1 = Strongly disagree, 5 = Strongly agree, required. **Use the exact statement text as the question title** - the analysis matches columns by it.

### Perceived efficiency (PE)
1. AI screening reduces the time I spend shortlisting candidates.
2. AI screening lets me handle more applications without lowering quality.
3. Using AI screening shortens our time-to-hire.
4. AI screening reduces repetitive manual work in recruitment.

### Trust in AI-shortlisted candidates (TR)
1. I trust candidates shortlisted by AI tools to be a good match for the role.
2. I would be comfortable relying on an AI ranking without re-checking every resume.
3. AI screening tools tend to overlook strong candidates. *(reverse-worded)*
4. AI shortlists are as reliable as those made by an experienced recruiter.

### Adoption concerns (higher = more concerned) (AC)
1. I worry that AI screening tools could be biased against certain groups of candidates.
2. Protecting candidate data privacy is a barrier to adopting AI screening.
3. Not knowing how an AI tool ranks candidates makes me reluctant to use it.
4. The cost or effort of setting up AI screening is a barrier for us.
5. Legal or compliance risks make me cautious about AI in hiring.

### Perceived fairness (PF)
1. AI screening treats candidates more consistently than manual screening.
2. AI screening reduces the influence of personal bias in shortlisting.
3. AI screening is fair to candidates regardless of their gender.
4. AI screening is fair to candidates regardless of their college or educational background.

### Perceived decision quality (DQ)
1. AI-supported shortlists improve the quality of the people we hire.
2. AI recommendations help me justify my shortlisting decisions.
3. AI screening helps me focus my time on the most promising candidates.

### Intention to adopt (AI)
1. I intend to use (or keep using) AI screening tools in the next 12 months.
2. I would recommend AI screening tools to colleagues.
3. My organisation is likely to expand its use of AI in recruitment.
